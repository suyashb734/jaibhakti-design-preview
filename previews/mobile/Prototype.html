I do not know my gotra, what should I do?

If you do not know your gotra, you can consider your gotra as Kashyap. Rishi Kashyap is a sage whose descendants are found in every community, which is why he is so widely revered. Pandit ji will take these details during the seva.

Don't know your gotra?

Many families don't remember their gotra. Tradition allows the pandit to use Kashyap gotra in that case, because Kashyap Rishi is considered the ancestor of all. We'll mark it as “Kashyap (assigned)” so you always know.
